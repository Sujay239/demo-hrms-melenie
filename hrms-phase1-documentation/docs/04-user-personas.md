# User Personas

## Purpose
Capture representative goals and risks.

## Platform Operations Administrator
Role: `SUPER_ADMIN`. Needs safe tenant lifecycle, consultant assignment and cross-tenant visibility. Highest risk is unintended platform-wide action.

## HR / Company Administrator
Role: `TENANT_ADMIN`. Needs efficient people/configuration/approval/document workflows. Must never cross tenant boundary.

## HR Consultant
Role: `CONSULTANT`. Needs clear current tenant and assigned-tenant switching. Assignment is a hard security boundary.

## Employee
Role: `EMPLOYEE`. Needs fast self-service for profile, attendance, leave, documents, notices, tickets, knowledge and rooms.

## New Hire
Role: `NEW_HIRE`. Needs a simple onboarding checklist, safe uploads, offer/acknowledgement workflow and visible verification. Must not see Employee modules before conversion.

## Accessibility persona
May depend on keyboard, zoom, assistive technology or reduced motion. No critical action may be hover-only or color-only.
