# EU Readiness Architecture

## Purpose
Prepare Phase 1 for future European privacy/regional functionality without claiming compliance.

## Phase 1 readiness
- Region/country relationships.
- IANA timezone.
- Locale.
- Data ownership/associations.
- Auditability.
- Sensitive document classification.
- Retention-aware deletion modeling where needed.
- Storage abstraction capable of future regional strategy.

## Future implementation
Consent/lawful basis, privacy notices, retention automation, data access/export/correction/deletion, residency/transfer controls, processor governance, incident/breach procedures and legal validation.

## Statement
Phase 1 is **not claimed to be GDPR compliant**. Legal compliance requires product configuration, contracts, policies, operations, evidence and legal review beyond architecture.
